# Deadwax

A curated album player for macOS. Deadwax shows only the albums you add to your collection, with large artwork, credits and notes, and plays them through Spotify on any Spotify Connect device: Sonos, a TV, or the Spotify app.

It's built with Tauri 2 (Rust and React) and needs Spotify Premium. It's early: the plan lives in [`docs/rfcs`](docs/rfcs).

Deadwax isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify.
